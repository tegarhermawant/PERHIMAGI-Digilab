Panduan Hidrolika & Well Control

1. Volume & Kapasitas

Menghitung volume lubang dan anulus berdasarkan geometri sumur.

Kapasitas (bbl/ft):

\[ Kap = \frac{ID^2}{1029.4} \]

Volume Anulus:

\[ V_{an} = V_{hole} - V_{disp} \]

2. Sirkulasi & Hidrolika

Debit Pompa (Q):

\[ Q = POP \times SPM \]

Lag Time (Bottoms Up):

\[ T_{lag} = \frac{V_{anulus}}{Q} \]

Annular Velocity (AV):

\[ AV = \frac{24.5 \times Q_{gpm}}{D_{hole}^2 - D_{pipe}^2} \]

ECD:

\[ ECD = MW + \frac{APL}{0.052 \times TVD} \]

3. Well Control

Tekanan Formasi (Pf):

\[ Pf = (0.052 \times MW \times TVD) + SIDPP \]

Kill Mud Weight (KMW):

\[ KMW = \frac{Pf}{0.052 \times TVD} \]
